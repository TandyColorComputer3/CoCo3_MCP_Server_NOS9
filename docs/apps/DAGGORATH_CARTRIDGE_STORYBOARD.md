# Daggorath source-cartridge attract storyboard

This is a **reference for what the player sees and hears**, separate from game-state tests. It is a forensic specification, not acceptance of the present EOU demo. The original reference is the recovered source at `/Volumes/SEDONA/Projects/daggorath-reference`, commit `a94326f00ebb16a106b540c58bc2ccf5f7b66dac`. `DAGGORATH.ASM` was assembled with `lwasm --format=raw`; the resulting 8,192-byte ROM has SHA-256 `35e6a77354dcf1a3048f276824b7a0f9f759115fdd40603664cebfb3a7da6571`. This establishes the behavior of that **source-built ROM**. It does not independently authenticate a retail cartridge dump.

The capture used MAME 0.289 `coco3h`, 2 MB, RGB screen configuration `1`, without a guest disk. Its 190.028-second video has 11,387 frames at approximately 59.92 frames/s; it shows the whole first autonomous loop and the opening of the next. The full AVI/WAV and debugger logs remain disposable under `/private/tmp/daggorath-storyboard/`. The [curated frame manifest](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/frame-manifest.json), [stable four-row text changes](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-primary-text-events.csv), [command/state boundaries](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-command-boundaries.csv), [creature render calls](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-creature-draws.csv), [sound calls](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-sound-calls.csv), [heart edges](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-heart-edges.csv), and [upper-field raster IDs](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-curated-viewport-rasters.csv) are the compact retained observations. PNG names are numbered storyboard beats; each comes directly from the source-ROM video at the approximate capture time below.

**Time conventions.** `video s` below is elapsed emulated video time after MAME capture started, rounded to 0.1 s. The source debugger's `SECOND:TENTH:JIFFY` counter starts separately and wraps each minute; its exact jiffies are in the CSVs. The two independent runs have an approximately 1.4 s startup offset, so a video time is not asserted to be the same instant as a numeric source-counter time. A still is evidence for its own frame, not for everything between two stills. The original video and source routines, rather than an EOU CCB alone, establish player-visible beats.

## Source control flow and four-row text model

`ONCE.ASM:COMINI` initializes the display and emits the inverse `COPYRIGHT  DYNA MICRO  MCMLXXXII` status material. `DEMO10` enables autoplay, calls the crescent Wizard `WIZIN0` with `D4.ASM:WIZ1`, emits two packed welcome strings, waits twice, calls `WIZOUT` and `ZFLOP`, then selects source demo level 2. `GAME20` shows `PREPARE!`, creates the source level and `DEMDAT` objects. `GAME40` shows the full map for two waits, then `GAME50 → PLOOK.ASM:INIVUX → MISC.ASM:PROMPX` clears to the initial dungeon and creates the command prompt. `HUMAN.ASM:PLAY20–PLAY50` supplies all 17 `TOKEN.ASM:AUTTAB` commands through the ordinary character/command path. At `AUTEND`, `PLAY20` waits twice and jumps back to `DEMO`, restarting Wizard and copyright. Source `COMMON.ASM:CLK50` checks keyboard input during autoplay and can instead start a fresh normal game; the prior controlled `X` test in [Attract M1](DAGGORATH_ATTRACT_M1.md) observed that transition at about 30 s. This no-input capture does not establish the first key-ready scanline or every possible key's behavior.

The prior source-built frame trace in [Attract M1](DAGGORATH_ATTRACT_M1.md) bounds the brief opening transitions more tightly than the representative PNGs. These are emulated video times, not new logical waits invented for the port:

`COMDAT.ASM:TXTPRI` defines **32 characters × four rows**, not a two-line message field. `COMTXT.ASM:TXTCR` moves down one 32-character row and homes the column. `TXTSER.ASM:TXTCHR` invokes `COMTXT.ASM:TXTSCR` when the 128-cell area fills; rows 1–3 move up and row 4 clears. `CLEAR.ASM:CLRPRX` clears all four. The rows remain visible until a clear or scroll. The source 5×7 glyph occupies an 8-pixel cell. There is no fifth primary row at y=192.

The [text-change CSV](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-primary-text-events.csv) decodes the source `SWCTAB` glyphs from **every one of the 11,387 video frames** in the 190-second capture and records 81 stable four-row changes (49 through the first loop), including partial words such as `.PULL _`. A change is retained after two matching frames with a bounded glyph-error score; a one-frame scanout/cursor transient could still be omitted. The source routines establish output order between samples.

### Command-state and geometry interpretation

The [command-boundary CSV](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-command-boundaries.csv) records **entry** to each command, then `AUTEND`, not an after-command snapshot. Its first entry is `(12,22;N), power 6048, RNG $756519`; the entry to command 10 is `(9,22;N), damage 16, RNG $F194D4`, with live G18 at `(9,22)`. The next entry confirms G18 dead and player power 6136. These state checkpoints accompany, but do not replace, the displayed corridor and Giant images above. The visual corridor changes at commands 5, 8, 9 and 11–17 are separately retained rather than folded into a single “AUTTAB ran” result.

The creature's **first contribution** is the tiny right-side change between 57.1 and 57.5 s, correlated with `VIEWER.ASM:CMRDRW` using CCB `$0506` and range 2. Human-recognizable Giant geometry begins around 61.4 s, grows at 61.8 and 67.7 s, is absent in a presented frame at 69.9 s although the CCB remains live, returns at close range at 72.7 s, remains during `.ATTACK RIGHT  !!!`, then disappears only after Combat M1. The 111–117 s Giant is another live CCB, not a resurrection of G18. The source's type-5 creature is Stone Giant 2 (`COMDAT.ASM` creature/audio tables and `VIEWER.ASM:VIEW30`). The [draw-call CSV](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-creature-draws.csv) preserves CCB address, range and vector pointer for each submitted Giant render; a submitted draw is not by itself proof of recognizability.

The 23 curated gameplay/view frames at beats 012–034 contain **19 distinct 512×152 upper-field pixel rasters** in this capture, measured with an identical `ffmpeg framemd5` crop and listed [here](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-curated-viewport-rasters.csv). This counts selected reference views, not all unique frames in the 59.92-fps run. Text and heart variations can create additional distinct full-screen frames.

## Heart and sound contract

`COMMON.ASM:CLK30–CLK32` decrements `HEARTC`; on zero it reloads from `HEARTR`, toggles PIA PB1 for sound, complements `HEARTS`, and draws the small/large two-glyph heart at status columns 15–16. The same IRQ event drives audible edge and logical/visible phase. The [128 first-loop edge observations](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-heart-edges.csv) show alternating `$FF/$00` phase. Early steady `HEARTR=$2E` gives 46-jiffy periods; during approach it is `$2D` (45); after command-10 combat it is `$29` (41). Screenshots show the heart inside the inverse status strip at logical y=152–159, not in the primary text rows. Presentation may lag an IRQ edge; this capture does not measure a frame-exact visual latency distribution.

`SOUNDS.ASM:SOUNDX` calls are retained in the [sound CSV](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/source-sound-calls.csv). Wizard has `KABOOM` calls at source counters about 5.2 and 9.3 s plus `COMMON.ASM:CLK20` fade buzz. Torch use calls `CHUCK` at 35.0 s. The nearby Stone Giant emits `GRAWL` type-5 cues at varying levels during approach; full-level calls are at 64.08 and 69.22 s source time, when the Giant is approaching/at the player. Player attack produces `WHOOSH` 73.95 → `KLINK` 74.10 → `BANG` 74.50, while the Giant is still displayed at attack text entry and before the post-kill redraw. The [25-second original Giant/combat audio excerpt](assets/daggorath-cartridge-storyboard/giant-approach-combat-original.mp3) retains those audible phases without the 190-second raw WAV. Other original calls in the first loop include `KKLANK` (type 7) and `PSSST` (type 6); the source table supplies the identities, while this run's debugger log supplies their occurrence. These are cartridge DAC/source events, not evidence that EOU SSC recipes sound identical.

The current EOU success-to-`OK` policy is visible in `apps/daggorath/src/gameplay/command-overlay.c:message_for` and, for example, the [M2 autoplay EOU frame](assets/daggorath-attract-visual-m2/m2-final-autoplay-eou.png). These eight current EOU `OK` presentations are therefore cross-beat **EXTRA** findings. Original `EXAMINE`/`LOOK` are separate view states and need their own comparison; the statement is not a blanket ban on every possible source message.

The current EOU M3 [isolated renderer test](DAGGORATH_DYNAMIC_PRESENTATION_M3.md) reports only 4, 0, 2 and 6 distinct **logical** pixels from G18 at commands 6–9. This supports a **visible-geometry fidelity gap**. It does not yet distinguish whether the cause is missing vector geometry, scaling, transform, occlusion, or presentation timing; that requires a matched EOU pre-command-10 capture at the same source state. Do not infer such a cause from CCB existence or these sparse EOU stills.
